Will / won't + Verb 1

When do we use it?

  • Predictions (based on beliefs or opinions): I think it will rain tomorrow.
  • Future facts (unchangeable future events): The sun will rise at 6:45 AM.
  • Instant decisions: I'm tired. I'll go to bed now.
  • Promises: I won't tell anyone your secret.
  • Offers & suggestions: Shall I help you with your bags? / Shall we go out tonight?

Positive: Subject + will + verb1

Negative: Subject + won't + verb1

Be going to + verb 1

  • For planned actions (decided before the moment of speaking): I'm going to start a new course next month.
  • For predictions with evidence: Look at those dark clouds! It's going to rain.

Positive: Subject + am/is/are + going to + verb1

Negative: Subject + am/is/are + not + going to + verb1

Present Continuous for future arrangements

Scheduled plans with a specific time or arrangement:

  • I'm meeting my friend at 6 PM.
  • We're flying to Italy next week.
  • She's having dinner with her boss tomorrow.
FormUsed forExample
WillInstant decisions, promises, predictionsI'll call you later.
Be Going toPlans and evidence-based predictionsIt's going to snow. Look at the sky!
Present ContinuousArranged events or appointmentsI'm seeing the doctor on Friday.

Future in the past

We always use was / were going to to talk about failed plans.

I was going to visit my uncle but he was abroad.

They were going to meet in café but he had an important meeting so they postponed it.

Choosing the best future form

  • Use will for instant decisions, promises, offers and opinion-based predictions.
  • Use be going to for intentions decided before speaking and predictions with visible evidence.
  • Use the present continuous for fixed arrangements with people, places or times already organized.
  • Use shall mainly with I / we for offers and suggestions: Shall I open the window? Shall we start?

🧑‍🏫 Ravza için detaylı anlatım

Bu konuda asıl hedef: will, be going to, present continuous ve future in the past yapılarını bağlama göre seçmek.

Gelecek anlatırken tek bir yapı yoktur. Will konuşma anındaki karar, söz, teklif ve fikir tahmini verir. Going to önceden niyet veya görünen kanıtı anlatır. Present Continuous ayarlanmış planı anlatır.

1. Önce anlamı yakala
I think = opinion prediction; Look at those clouds = evidence; tomorrow at 6 with a person = arrangement.
2. Sonra yapıyı kur
will + V1, am/is/are going to + V1, am/is/are + V-ing, was/were going to + V1.
3. En son kontrol et
Karar şimdi mi verildi, daha önce mi planlandı, yoksa takvime mi bağlı?

🧩 Soru çözerken adım adım

  • Anlık karar, söz veya teklif varsa will seç.
  • Görünen kanıt ya da önceden karar varsa going to seç.
  • Saat/yer/kişiyle organize plan varsa Present Continuous kullan.
  • Geçmişte planlandı ama olmadıysa was/were going to kullan.

⚠️ Ravza'nın dikkat etmesi gereken hata

Tipik hata: Her gelecek cümlesine will koymak.

Doğru düşünme: Önce bağlamı oku: plan mı, kanıt mı, anlık karar mı, fixed arrangement mı?

🎯 Konu sonunda kendine sor

  • Evidence var mı?
  • Plan önceden mi yapılmış?
  • Bu konuşma anındaki karar mı?

🧑‍🏫 Profesyonel öğretmen anlatımı

Kaynak will/won't yapısını predictions, future facts, instant decisions, promises, offers ve suggestions için verir. Be going to ise plans and intentions ve evidence-based predictions için kullanılır. Present Continuous ayarlanmış gelecek planlarda geçer. Future in the past için was/were going to kullanılır.

  • 📌 will: belief/opinion prediction, future fact, instant decision, promise, offer.
  • 📌 shall I / shall we: offer ve suggestion.
  • 📌 be going to: önceden planlanmış niyet veya görünen kanıta dayalı tahmin.
  • 📌 Present Continuous: arranged future plan.
  • 📌 was/were going to: geçmişteki gerçekleşmeyen plan.

✅ Kaynak örnekleriyle anlatım

English: I'm tired. I'll go to bed now.

Türkçe: Yorgunum. Şimdi yatacağım.

Öğretmen notu: Karar konuşma anında veriliyor; will.

English: Look at those dark clouds! It's going to rain.

Türkçe: Şu kara bulutlara bak! Yağmur yağacak.

Öğretmen notu: Görünen kanıt var; going to.

English: Shall we go out tonight?

Türkçe: Bu akşam dışarı çıkalım mı?

Öğretmen notu: Shall we suggestion verir.

⚠️ Sık yapılan hatalar

Yanlış: Look at those clouds! It will rain. (kanıt vurgusunda)

Doğru: Look at those clouds! It's going to rain.

Neden? Kaynakta görünen kanıta dayalı tahmin için going to verilir.

Yanlış: I was will call you.

Doğru: I was going to call you.

Neden? Future in the past yapısı was/were going to ile kurulur.
