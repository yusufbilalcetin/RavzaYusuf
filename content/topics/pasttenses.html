Past Simple

We use the past simple to talk about finished actions that happened in the past.

Form: Subject Pronoun + was/were + adjective/noun

Form: Subject Pronoun + Verb 2 (regular or irregular)

She was tired after the long trip.

They were at the museum yesterday.

I visited my grandparents last weekend.

He wrote a fantastic story when he was younger.

NOTE: We do NOT use Verb 2 in questions or negatives!

Not correct: Did you went to school yesterday?

Correct: Did you go to school yesterday?

Past Continuous

We use the past continuous to describe an action that was interrupted by another action or an action that was happening at a specific time in the past.

Form: Subject Pronoun + was/were + verb-ing

  • I was reading a book when the phone rang.
  • She was cooking dinner at 6 PM.
  • They were playing football while it started raining.
  • What were you doing at 10 o'clock last night?

Used to

We use used to to talk about things we did regularly in the past but don't do anymore.

  • Positive: Subject + used to + verb 1
  • Negative: Subject + didn't use to + verb 1
  • Question: Did + subject + use to + verb 1

Don't use "used to" for short-term past events.

Incorrect: I used to live in Italy (for only two months).

Correct: I lived in Italy for two months.

We can also use an adverb of frequency + Verb 2 instead of used to:

  • Tom often walked to school.
  • I never liked broccoli, but now I love it.
  • We sometimes visited our cousins on weekends.

Past simple vs past continuous vs used to

  • Past simple gives the finished action: The lesson ended at nine.
  • Past continuous gives the background or interrupted action: We were revising when the bell rang.
  • Used to describes old habits or states that are no longer true: I used to play tennis every weekend.
  • In negatives and questions with used to, use use after did / didn't: Did you use to walk to school?

🧑‍🏫 Ravza için detaylı anlatım

Bu konuda asıl hedef: Past Simple, Past Continuous ve used to yapılarını olay sırasına göre seçmek.

Geçmiş zaman sorularında önce olayın bitti mi, bir anda mı oldu, yoksa arka planda devam mı ediyordu diye düşün. Used to ise artık doğru olmayan eski alışkanlıkları anlatır.

1. Önce anlamı yakala
Past Simple bitmiş olay; Past Continuous geçmişte devam eden arka plan; used to eski alışkanlık/eski durum.
2. Sonra yapıyı kur
Did ile soru/olumsuzda fiil yalınlaşır. Past Continuous: was/were + V-ing.
3. En son kontrol et
Cümlede when/while var mı? Biri uzun eylem, biri bölen kısa eylem olabilir.

🧩 Soru çözerken adım adım

  • Belirli geçmiş zaman varsa Past Simple düşün.
  • Geçmişte belirli anda devam eden eylem varsa Past Continuous kullan.
  • Artık olmayan eski alışkanlık için used to kullan.
  • Did/didn't sonrası V2 kullanma; fiili V1 yap.

⚠️ Ravza'nın dikkat etmesi gereken hata

Tipik hata: Did you went? veya didn't used to demek.

Doğru düşünme: Did gelirse fiil V1 olur: Did you go? / didn't use to.

🎯 Konu sonunda kendine sor

  • Olay tamamlandı mı?
  • Arka plan eylemi var mı?
  • Bu eski ama artık yok olan bir alışkanlık mı?

🧑‍🏫 Profesyonel öğretmen anlatımı

Kaynakta Past Simple bitmiş geçmiş eylemler için, Past Continuous geçmişte belirli anda devam eden eylem için, used to ise artık doğru olmayan eski alışkanlık ve durumlar için veriliyor. Did ile soru kurduğunda fiili V2 yapmazsın; bu en önemli sınav hatasıdır.

  • 📌 Past Simple: finished past action.
  • 📌 Past Continuous: was/were + V-ing, geçmişte devam eden eylem.
  • 📌 Used to: geçmiş alışkanlık veya eski durum; artık geçerli olmayabilir.
  • 📌 Did/didn't sonrası fiil V1 olur.
